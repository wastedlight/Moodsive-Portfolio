## 🌱 About Moodsive

**Moodsive** is a personal mental wellness application designed to give people a private space to understand their emotions, track their moods and express their thoughts.

The idea behind Moodsive is simple: looking after your mental wellbeing shouldn't require giving away your personal data.

Unlike platforms that rely on accounts and cloud-based storage, Moodsive is built around a local-first approach. Your journal entries and mood information stay on your device.

No accounts. No cloud sync. No unnecessary tracking.

Just you and your journey.

## ✨ Features

### 💚 Mood Tracking

Keep track of how you're feeling and build a better understanding of your emotional patterns.

- Daily mood tracking
- Mood history
- Visual mood trends
- Identify patterns over time
- Reflect on changes in your wellbeing

### 📖 Private Journaling

A personal space to write freely, process your thoughts and reflect on your experiences.

- Private journal entries
- Personal reflections
- Review previous entries
- Locally stored journal data

### 📊 Mood Analytics

Turn your mood history into meaningful insights.

- Visual mood charts
- Historical mood trends
- Emotional pattern tracking
- Personal reflection over time

### 🧠 Support Resources

Access helpful mental wellness resources and coping strategies.

Designed to provide additional support and encourage healthy self-reflection.

### 🎨 Personalisation

Moodsive is designed to provide a comfortable and welcoming experience, with customisation and themes forming part of the application.

## 🔒 Privacy First

Privacy isn't an optional extra in Moodsive. It's one of the core principles behind the application.

| Privacy Feature | Approach |
|---|---|
| Data Storage | Stored locally on your device |
| Cloud Sync | No cloud syncing |
| User Accounts | No sign-up required |
| Tracking | No tracking |
| Personal Journals | Remain on your device |

Your personal thoughts and mood history belong to you.
